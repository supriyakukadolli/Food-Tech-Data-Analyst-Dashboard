# Food-Tech-Data-Analyst-Dashboard
I have developed a comprehensive project in PowerBI, creating multiple dashboard and tables to analyze the data. This process involves the several stages, including preprocessing and dashboard.
📋 Project Overview: 

This project provides a  analysis of the global Food-Tech industry, specifically focusing on restaurant performance, consumer behavior, and market trends across 15 countries and 141 cities.

By leveraging a dataset of over 9,551 restaurants, I built three distinct dashboard solutions (Power BI, Tableau, and Excel) to demonstrate how different BI tools can be used to derive actionable insights such as identifying profitable price buckets, popular cuisines, and high-growth geographical regions.

🎯 Short Description/Purpose:

The Food-Tech Analysis Dashboard is designed for stakeholders in the food delivery and hospitality sectors. Its primary purpose is to:

Analyze Market Penetration: Track restaurant counts and openings across different years and quarters.

Identify Consumer Preferences: Evaluate average ratings, total votes, and the popularity of specific cuisines (e.g., North Indian, Chinese).

Operational Insights: Compare restaurants offering Online Delivery (25.66%) vs. Table Booking (12.12%) to understand service gaps.

Price Sensitivity: Segment the market into "Price Buckets" to see where the highest volume of consumer engagement lies.

💻 Tech Stack:

Power BI: Utilized for advanced data modeling, DAX for calculated measures and interactive map visuals.

Tableau: Focused on high-level executive sales analysis, utilizing tree maps for restaurant openings and global distribution maps.

Microsoft Excel: Built a fully interactive dashboard using Pivot Tables, Slicers, and Dynamic Charts to demonstrate robust reporting without specialized software.

Power Query: Used across tools for the ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) process to clean null values and standardize currency/country names.

📊 Data Source:

The underlying dataset consists of 9,551 records representing restaurants worldwide. 
Key attributes include:

Geographical: Country, City, Address, and Coordinates.

Operational: Has Online Delivery, Has Table Booking, Price Range.

Financial: Average Cost for Two, Currency.

Performance: Aggregate Rating, Rating Color, and Vote Count.

✨ Features/Highlights:

1. Multi-Platform Implementation
Power BI Dashboard: Features a "Main View" for high-level KPIs and a "Detailed View" using Waterfall Charts for cuisine analysis and Funnel Charts for price bucket distribution.

Tableau Analysis: Highlights "Year-wise Sales" and "Country-wise Average Sale" using sophisticated heat maps.

Excel Dashboard: Includes user-friendly Country and Year Slicers and a "Top 5 Cuisines" donut chart for quick decision-making.

2. Business Insights & KPIs
Market Snapshot: Quickly identifies that India leads the restaurant count with 8,652 outlets.

Service Analysis: Visualizes that while 74% of restaurants lack online delivery, those that have it contribute significantly to the total vote count.

Trend Tracking: A line chart showing restaurant openings from 2010 to 2018, identifying 2017–2018 as a period of significant recovery/growth.

3. Advanced Visualizations
Geographical Mapping: Global maps identifying restaurant density in North America, Asia, and Europe.

Cuisine Popularity: A breakdown showing "North Indian" as the dominant cuisine  followed by "Chinese" and "Fast Food."

Rating Distribution: Correlating city-wise restaurant counts with the Sum of Ratings to identify quality hubs like New Delhi.
